🧪 Quick Test

Step 1: Go to Keycloak Login

Open Keycloak Login

Step 2: Test with Direct URLs

Copy these URLs into your browser address bar:

http://localhost:8080/realms/master/account?error=cancelledOnApp
http://localhost:8080/realms/master/account?error=access_denied

Step 3: Test with Wrong Login

  1. Go to: http://localhost:8080/realms/master/account
  2. Enter wrong username: wronguser
  3. Enter wrong password: wrongpass
  4. Click Login
  5. Should redirect to your error page!

Debug Info

Open browser console (F12) and look for [ErrorRedirect] messages.

Expected redirect: https://app.valura.co.in/auth?error_code=...
